About the Workshop

Jai SriRam Welding Works

Run by S. Harish, Jai SriRam Welding Works is a trusted local fabrication workshop specializing in iron gates, grills, sheds, railings and all types of custom welding works. Every piece is hand-crafted with care, durability and elegant finishing.

Located near Soan Road, Village Laxman Chanda, Nirmal - 504106, we serve homes, farms, shops and industries with reliable, on-time fabrication service.

  • ✔ Custom Iron & Steel Fabrication
  • ✔ On-site Measurement & Free Estimate
  • ✔ Rust-proof Primer & Paint Finish
  • ✔ Timely Delivery & Installation
